Opioids represent a broad category of compounds
that can have a variety of applications.

One major application of opioids is the management of moderate to severe pain. These drugs have high addiction potentials, however, making misuse of these compounds a cause for concern. There can be serious medical consequences to opioid intoxication or overdose.
